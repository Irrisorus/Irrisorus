Fullstack developer
-------------------

Hey, I am Yaroslav, an 18-year-old Full-Stack Developer with a deep passion for building fast, reliable, and scalable web applications. My journey into tech started back in childhood when my parents enrolled me in the IT STEP Academy. What began as early curiosity quickly turned into a lifelong obsession with engineering, leading me to write code, design backend systems, and solve complex logic puzzles every single day.

Today, I specialize in TypeScript, React, Next.js, and NestJS, with a heavy emphasis on robust backend architectures. I have commercial experience crafting end-to-end production apps, high-throughput REST APIs, custom authentication engines, relational database schemas, and Telegram Mini Apps. Whether it is taking full ownership of an isolated microservice, executing zero-downtime database migrations, or designing smooth user interfaces, I love taking real responsibility and turning messy problems into clean, practical code.

When I am not shipping products or diving into modern software architecture, I am constantly exploring new technologies, refining my engineering practices, and looking for challenging problems to tackle. Always open to connecting, collaborating on ambitious projects, or discussing modern web dev!
